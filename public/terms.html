CrewCast Terms & SMS Program Terms

Last updated: April 22, 2026

About the service

CrewCast is an employee shift-scheduling app operated at crewcast-app-production.up.railway.app. Your employer signs up for CrewCast and invites you as an employee. You use the app to see your schedule, respond to shifts, request swaps, and communicate with your employer.

SMS Program Name and Description

Program name: CrewCast Notifications.

Description: CrewCast sends employees text messages about their work schedule — shift reminders, new schedule publishes, shift-swap requests, schedule changes, and replies to messages you send to your employer through the app.

Message frequency

Message frequency varies based on your schedule and your employer's activity. Most employees receive roughly 1–10 messages per week.

Message & data rates

Message and data rates may apply. Your mobile carrier may charge you for sending or receiving text messages. CrewCast does not charge employees for messages.

HELP and STOP instructions

For help, reply HELP to any CrewCast text message, or email mdunker04@gmail.com.

To stop receiving CrewCast text messages at any time, reply STOP to any CrewCast text message. You will receive one final confirmation message and then no further messages. To resume messages later, reply START.

How you opt in

Your employer enters your phone number in CrewCast when they invite you. You then receive a one-time setup link. By completing account setup you consent to receive shift and schedule text messages from CrewCast at that number. You can opt out at any time with STOP.

Eligibility

You must be 13 years or older to use CrewCast. Participation in the SMS program requires a working mobile phone number on a carrier that supports text messaging in the U.S.

Support

Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.
