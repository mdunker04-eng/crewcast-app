CrewCast SMS Program

Last updated: April 28, 2026

This page describes the CrewCast SMS Notifications program, including who receives messages, how opt-in works, the kinds of messages sent, message frequency, and how to get help or stop messages.

Program name

CrewCast Notifications

Who receives messages

Employees of small businesses (currently apple orchards, breweries, restaurants, and similar shift-based operators) that use CrewCast to manage employee scheduling. Employees do not sign up directly on a public website. Their employer signs up for CrewCast, agrees to our Terms & SMS Program Terms, and adds employees by entering each employee's name and mobile phone number. Each employee then receives a one-time SMS setup link and confirms enrollment by tapping that link and completing account setup.

How opt-in works (Call to Action)

There are two opt-in moments where the employee gives clear, affirmative consent:

  1. Activation link. When an employer adds an employee, the employee receives a single SMS with a one-time setup link. The message includes program identification (CrewCast), the purpose (account setup for shift scheduling), and standard HELP/STOP language. Tapping the link and completing account setup is the employee's affirmative opt-in to receive shift-related text messages from CrewCast at that phone number.
  2. Self-service login. Returning employees can request a sign-in link at /login by entering their phone number and tapping "Text me a sign-in link." The button is preceded by a visible consent disclosure stating that tapping it will send an SMS, that message and data rates may apply, and that STOP/HELP work. Tapping the button is affirmative consent to receive that single transactional sign-in message.
Sample consent line shown next to opt-in buttons:
"By tapping the button you agree to receive shift-related text messages from CrewCast at the phone number above. Msg & data rates may apply. Reply STOP to unsubscribe, HELP for help."

What messages employees receive

  • One-time setup / sign-in links (transactional)
  • New schedule published
  • Shift reminders
  • Schedule change notifications
  • Shift swap requests and approvals
  • Two-way replies between employees and their employer through the in-app inbox

CrewCast does not send marketing, promotional, or third-party messages.

Message frequency

Roughly 1–10 messages per week per employee, depending on their schedule and their employer's activity.

Message and data rates

Message and data rates may apply. Your mobile carrier may charge you for sending or receiving text messages. CrewCast does not charge employees for messages.

HELP

For help, reply HELP to any CrewCast text message, or email mdunker04@gmail.com.

STOP

To stop receiving CrewCast text messages at any time, reply STOP to any CrewCast text message. You will receive one final confirmation message and then no further messages. To resume messages later, reply START.

Privacy

CrewCast does not share or sell phone numbers. SMS opt-in consent is never shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing purposes. Carriers receive the phone number and message content only to deliver the SMS. Full details: Privacy Policy.
