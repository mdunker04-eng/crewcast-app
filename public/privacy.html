CrewCast Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 22, 2026

CrewCast is an employee shift-scheduling tool. This policy explains what we collect and how we use it.

What we collect

When your employer (the business administrator) invites you to CrewCast, they enter your first and last name and your mobile phone number. When you set up your account we store those details along with your assigned shifts, availability, and any messages you send or receive through the app. We do not collect Social Security numbers, financial information, or government IDs.

How we use your information

We use your phone number and account details only to operate the scheduling service for your employer — sending you shift schedules, reminders, swap requests, and replies to messages you send us. We do not use your information for advertising.

SMS messaging

Phone numbers and SMS opt-in consent are never shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing purposes. Carriers (e.g., AT&T, Verizon, T-Mobile) receive the phone number and message content only to deliver the SMS, as required by the underlying SMS service provider.

Who we share with

We share data only with service providers needed to run the app — our SMS provider (Twilio), our hosting provider (Railway), and our database — and only to the extent needed to deliver your shift information. We do not sell your personal information.

Retention and deletion

Your data is retained while you have an active account. You can request deletion by replying STOP to any CrewCast text message, or by asking your employer to remove your account.

Your choices

You can stop receiving CrewCast text messages at any time by replying STOP. You can get help information by replying HELP.
