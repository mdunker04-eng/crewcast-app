Getting Started Checklist

1
Log in & complete Settings

Go to Settings and fill in your business details, operating hours, and scheduling rules.

2
Set up your stations

Add the work areas where employees will be assigned (e.g., Front Desk, Kitchen, Register).

3
Add your employees

Add team members and assign them to the stations they're trained on. Each employee gets an invite link to set up their own account.

4
Create your first schedule

Pick a date range and use Auto-Fill to intelligently assign employees based on availability, training, and preferences.

5
Share with your team

Employees can install CrewCast on their phones to view schedules, set availability, and manage shift swaps.

📲 Install on Your Phone
